OpenCADIrrigation brings CAD drawing, the hydraulic network and the calculation together: trace the area, lay out laterals, valves and the mainline, press F5 and get pipe sizes, pressures, uniformity, operating shifts, a bill of materials and printable reports. Available in 20 languages, following your computer's language.

What OpenCADIrrigation does

  • Full CAD drawing

    Lines, polylines, arcs, splines, hatches, dimensions, text and layers, with snaps, window selection, grips and AutoCAD-style shortcuts (L, PL, C, D, ZE…).

  • Hydraulic network

    Water supplies, pumps, mainline, zone valves, laterals with emitters, drip tapes and driplines, filters, pressure regulators, meters and junctions; lateral-block and sprinkler-grid generators.

  • Validated hydraulic engine

    Hazen-Williams and Darcy-Weisbach, minor losses, emitters q = k·Hˣ, network solver by the Global Gradient Algorithm (as in EPANET), pump curves and operating point, water hammer and flushing analysis.

  • Automatic sizing

    Linear programming or maximum velocity, honouring the emitter pressure window and pipe pressure classes, trading pipe cost against energy, telescoping diameters and operating shifts.

  • Terrain and relief

    Contours, spot heights, a digital terrain model by triangulation, elevation downloaded from the internet at a coordinate, longitudinal profiles and the hydraulic grade line over the ground.

  • Reports and materials

    Zones, pipes, emitters, uniformity (CU, DU, EU), crop water requirements, controller programme and a bill of materials with fittings; export to PDF, Excel, CSV and TXT.

  • File exchange

    Imports DXF, DWG, KML/KMZ, SHP, CSV/XYZ, GeoTIFF and georeferenced images; exports DXF, DWG, PDF sheets, SVG, KML, KMZ and GeoJSON.

  • Manufacturer catalogues

    Manufacturer catalogues in JSON (Netafim, Rivulis, Jain, Toro, Rain Bird, Nelson, Hunter, NaanDanJain, Senninger, Irritec, Bermad, Schneider, Amanco Wavin, Multilit) ready to use, plus your own packs loaded from a file or a URL.

How it works

  1. Draw or import the area. Bring a DXF, DWG, KML or a satellite image, or draw the field, garden or pitch directly. Add contours or download the elevation from a coordinate.
  2. Build the network. Generate lateral blocks or sprinkler grids in one click, place valves, pump and water supply and connect everything with the mainline.
  3. Design and document. Press F5: the app sizes zones and mainline, checks pressures and velocities and produces reports, the bill of materials and the PDF sheet.

Frequently asked questions

What is OpenCADIrrigation?
OpenCADIrrigation is a free web application for designing pressurised irrigation systems: a CAD with a hydraulic engine that lays out the network, sizes the pipes, checks pressures and uniformity and produces the bill of materials and reports, from a home garden to a centre pivot.
Is OpenCADIrrigation free?
Yes. It is free for professional, academic and personal use, with no sign-up.
Who is it for?
Agricultural and landscape irrigation designers, agronomists and engineers, equipment dealers, teachers and students. The interactive tutorial and 15 ready examples let you start without reading a manual.
Do I need to install anything?
No. It runs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop, tablet and phone, and can be installed as an app (PWA) to open in its own window.
Are my projects stored in the cloud?
No. Projects are saved in your browser and as .oci.json files you keep wherever you like. Only the online elevation and the base maps reach the internet, when you ask for them.
How does it calculate the hydraulics?
Friction losses by Hazen-Williams or Darcy-Weisbach, emitters by q = k·Hˣ and the whole network solved by the Global Gradient Algorithm, the method used by EPANET. Pipes are sized by linear programming within the emitter pressure window, and the design also checks uniformity (CU, DU, EU), water hammer and flushing.
Does it work with AutoCAD and other CADs?
Yes. It imports and exports DXF and DWG (AutoCAD 2000 to 2018), plus Google Earth KML/KMZ, shapefiles, GeoJSON, SVG and PDF.
Which languages are available?
Twenty, the same as this site (the list is at the foot of the page). The app opens in your computer's language and you can switch at any time under View → Language.
